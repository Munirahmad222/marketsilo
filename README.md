# MarketSilo - Financial & Market Tools Dashboard

MarketSilo is a clean, minimal, and modern financial platform designed to provide real-time market data without the noise. Built on public market data feeds, it offers seven focused tracking tools with zero clutter, allowing users to monitor assets in their preferred currency.

## Key Features
- **Stock Tracker:** Search any ticker, manage a personal watchlist, and view price history from 1 day up to 5 years.
- **Crypto Tracker:** Monitor the top 50 cryptocurrencies with 1h/24h/7d moves, sparklines, 30-day charts, and a built-in fiat converter.
- **Gold Price Tracker:** Track spot gold in your local currency by ounce, gram, 10g, tola, and kilo across multiple purities (24K, 22K, 21K, and 18K).
- **Silver & Metals Tracker:** View live silver, platinum, and palladium unit tables alongside the gold-to-silver ratio.
- **World Indices & Currencies:** Stay updated with global market indices and real-time exchange rates.
- **Financial Calculators:** Multi-purpose calculators optimized for quick asset valuations.
